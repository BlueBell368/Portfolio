Background

Last Updated 2026

My background was originally in English Education (teaching). Due to having to work full-time while going to full-time school (no parental financial support), I was drawn to the accessibility of online learning.

I loved the accessibility and flexibility of not having to sit through a class at a specific time.

This was early days for e-learning, though (Blackboard days, for example), and the design and organization were confusing to navigate.

I knew online learning was bound to expand over the years. My focus shifted away from in-person K-12 schools and to e-learning while in my final years of my bachelor's.

I moved cross-country from northern Illinois to Florida to pursue the major and internships in the e-learning field.

After spending a decade in various high-pressure, high-stakes contract jobs, my interest shifted to health and wellness.
