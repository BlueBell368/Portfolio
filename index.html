Introduction

My background originally was in English Education (teaching). As an independent, first-generation student, I had to work full time while going to school full time. So naturally, any option for online classes, I snatched up. I loved the accessibility and flexibility of not having to sit through a class at a specific time.

This was early days for e-learning, though (Blackboard days, for example), and the design and organization were confusing to navigate.

I knew the accessibility of online learning would make it expand over the years, and I loved the idea of using my experiences with bad design to help shape better-designed online courses such as easier navigation, clear direction, and user-friendly interfaces. So I looked up where such a major existed and saved up to try to move cross-country from Illinois to Florida, where a school that had such a major existed.

After spending a decade in various high-pressure, high-stakes contract jobs, stress began to wear down my motivation and interest in the field.

I saw a decline in my performance and my body's health. My interests shifted to health and wellness.
